# Creator Checklist — 8 steps from idea to posting

Print it, tick it, every video.

1. **Idea** — pick one from the Story Idea Library (or brainstorm 10 new ones with `bmad-brainstorming`). Check: new cast? new place? one visual symbol everyone understands instantly?
2. **Hook** — first line under 2 seconds, from the 8 hook formulas. The title card says the same thing.
3. **Script** — 12–18 scenes, 90–130 s. Setup → the rule → the outsider → two foreshadowing moments → crisis → twist → emotional payoff → comment question. Test it with `bmad-party-mode` ("would you keep watching at second 3, 30, 60?").
4. **Voices** — narrator `af_jessica` (fast, bright), one distinct voice per character. Plan pauses and emphasis (`voice-performance-director`). Listen to one sample line per voice.
5. **World + characters** — build the world from the Environment Reference Pack, the cast from the Character Reference Pack. Every character dressed differently. Faces cheated 30–45° toward the camera.
6. **Shots** — new angle every 1.5–2.5 s; close-ups with `CU()`; captions 1–2 words inside the safe zone (x 90–990, y 120–1600).
7. **Check** — test stills every 1.5 s, fix, full render, then review the FINISHED video at 1 frame per second. Loudness −14 LUFS.
8. **Publish** — thumbnail with big text (3–4 words), caption + 3–5 hashtags, pinned comment question, switch on the "AI-generated content" label, post at your best time, reply to the first comments within an hour.
