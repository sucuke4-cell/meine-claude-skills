# Story Idea Library — 30 original stories + 8 hook formulas

Every idea comes with a hook line (first 2 seconds), the twist, a suggested cast and a world. All ideas are original. Rule: every new video gets a NEW cast and a NEW place.

## The 8 hook formulas
1. **"What if …?"** — a strange rule everyone can picture. *"What if every friendship was a string you could see?"*
2. **"Everyone had X. Except one."** — the outsider. *"Every kid walked in at 100%. But not Eli."*
3. **"Never do X."** — a forbidden rule. *"Rule number one: never open the red door."*
4. **"This is the last time …"** — a countdown that creates urgency. *"He had three words left in his whole life."*
5. **"Nobody believed her, until …"** — the doubted hero.
6. **Show the ending first** — open on the most dramatic frame, then *"Three days earlier…"*
7. **A question the viewer must answer** — *"Would you keep the light burning for a stranger?"*
8. **A number that hurts** — *"She had zero friends. Or did she?"*
