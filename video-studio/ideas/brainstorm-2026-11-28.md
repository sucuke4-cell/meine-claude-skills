# Brainstorm: next emotional "What if" story (Ideate-for-me, bmad-brainstorming)
Goal: ~2 min English short, rounded avatars, NEW town + cast, one strong VISUAL symbol everyone can read instantly (like the battery HUD), bittersweet/wholesome twist, comment-bait question.

## Technique 1: "Make the invisible visible" (feelings/states as on-screen objects)
1. Everyone's lies grow as a visible nose-length number above their head.
2. Your loneliness shows as rain clouds only over you.
3. Kindness leaves glowing footprints that last a week.
4. Every unsaid "I love you" floats as a paper heart stuck above you.
5. Your remaining time with each person shows as a countdown when you look at them.
6. Worries appear as small grey stones in your backpack — people see how heavy it is.
7. Hearts show as glass jars filling with light when someone is loved.
8. Your voice volume is a visible bar — quiet kids are literally invisible below 10%.
9. Every friendship is a colored string tying wrists together.
10. Your shadow shows who you really are.
11. Your hair turns grey for every secret you keep.
12. Everyone has a "luck meter"; one girl's is always 0.
13. Your house glows brighter the happier the family inside is.
14. Plants grow wherever you smile.
15. Your age shows as how old you FEEL, not how old you are.

## Technique 2: Reversal (flip a normal rule)
16. What if kids raised their parents?
17. What if you were born old and grew younger (a kid who will become a baby next year)?
18. What if your memories could be sold at a shop?
19. What if gifts disappear when you stop appreciating them?
20. What if the town's weather follows the mood of one boy?
21. What if pets chose their owners at a "human shelter"?
22. What if your reflection lived the life you didn't choose?
23. What if you only got one "sorry" in your whole life?
24. What if words you speak cost coins?
25. What if every person can only say 1000 words in their life (a counter above head)?
26. What if birthdays only happen when someone remembers you?
27. What if your name disappears when no one says it anymore?
28. What if snow only fell on people who are sad?
29. What if your height changed with your confidence?
30. What if doors only opened for people who were truly welcome?

## Technique 3: Objects with a soul (one prop carries the story)
31. A paper airplane that always flies to the person who needs it most.
32. An umbrella that only keeps out sadness, not rain.
33. A music box that plays only when someone thinks of you.
34. A red scarf passed from person to person, each knitting one row.
35. A phone that receives messages from yourself 10 years later.
36. A lunchbox that always has an extra sandwich — for someone else.
37. A wristwatch that counts hugs, not hours.
38. A mailbox where letters to the dead get answered.
39. A streetlight that turns on only for lost kids.
40. A bench that remembers everyone who sat on it.

## Technique 4: Emotional core first (what feeling do we want in the comments?)
41. Grandma's memory as a glowing photo album; pages go blank one by one; grandson redraws them.
42. Dad works night shifts; his "time with you" bar is empty; son saves minutes in a jar.
43. Older brother pretends not to care; his string to little sister is the thickest in town.
44. Shy girl's voice bar at 1%; whole class whispers so she can be heard.
45. Kid sees everyone's "sad cloud"; his own is hidden under his hat.
46. A boy whose words counter hits 3 left: who gets his last words?
47. Mom's hair greys with every secret — the secret is how sick she was and she beat it.
48. A new kid with no name tag because no one says his name; class chants it.
49. Twins whose friendship string is cut by a fight; town gets colorless.
50. Old janitor's luck meter at 0 because he gave all his luck to students.

## Technique 5: Town worlds (new places to build)
51. Lantern Bay: floating-lantern harbor town.
52. Pinecrest: snowy mountain village with a cable car.
53. Sunnyvale Heights: desert town with wind chimes.
54. Cloverfield: farm town with a giant windmill + county fair.
55. Rainford: city where it always drizzles, neon umbrellas.
56. Coral Cove: beach town, boardwalk + aquarium.
57. Old Oak Row: autumn town, orange leaves, bakery, library.
58. Starfall: town under an observatory, night markets.
59. Bridgewater: town of many little bridges and canals.
60. Kitewood: hilltop town where everyone flies kites.

## Technique 6: Twist engine (the reveal that flips meaning)
61. The person with the "worst" meter is secretly the one powering everyone.
62. The counter wasn't counting down life — it counted down until they meet.
63. The cruel kid's string was cut because HE was protecting someone.
64. The voice you heard on the phone was your own, older and kind.
65. The town's sun is a lantern carried by one old man every morning.
66. The "empty" jar was full of invisible things: the hugs you never noticed.
67. Grandma remembered everything — she was pretending to forget so grandson would visit.
68. The rain cloud followed the girl because she cried for others.
69. The shop of memories was buying memories to give back to a sick mother.
70. The last word was "thank you", saved for the right person.

## Technique 7: Mash-ups (combine two of the above)
71. Words counter + Pinecrest snow: a boy with 3 words left before Christmas.
72. Friendship strings + Kitewood: strings become kite strings; the lost kite finds its kid.
73. Sad clouds + Rainford: a girl with an umbrella that shields others' clouds.
74. Memory album + Old Oak Row: autumn leaves are memories falling off.
75. Luck meter + Cloverfield fair: janitor's 0 luck; the fair wheel picks him.
76. Name disappearing + Bridgewater: names written on bridge locks.
77. Lantern Bay + unsaid I-love-yous: every unsaid word becomes a lantern drifting away.
78. Starfall + time-with-you countdown: stars fade when people stop visiting.
79. Coral Cove + mailbox to the dead: bottles from the sea get answers.
80. Kitewood + voice bar: shy girl whose kite only flies when she speaks.

## Technique 8: Hook-first lines (first 3 seconds)
81. "What if you could only say 1000 words in your whole life?"
82. "What if every friendship was a string you could see?"
83. "What if your sadness followed you as a rain cloud?"
84. "What if every 'I love you' you never said floated away as a lantern?"
85. "What if no one could see you unless they said your name?"
86. "What if your shadow showed who you really are?"
87. "What if you could see how much time you had left with someone?"
88. "What if hugs were the only way to stay warm in winter?"
89. "What if luck was a number everyone could see?"
90. "What if your house glowed only when your family was happy?"
...(plus ~20 variants of the hooks above; mined out)
